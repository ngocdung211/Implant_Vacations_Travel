Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about dental tourism or the implant process? We have answered the most common ones below. Don't see yours? Contact us for a free, no-obligation consultation.

1. Is the quality of dental care in Hanoi comparable to Japan?

Yes. Our partner clinics use globally recognised implant brands (such as Straumann and Nobel Biocare) and employ dentists trained to international standards. The clinics hold ISO certifications and follow strict sterilisation and safety protocols. Many of our dentists have undertaken advanced training in Europe, the US, and Asia.

2. How much can I really save compared to getting implants in Japan?

A single dental implant in Japan typically costs ¥300,000–¥500,000 (approximately USD 2,000–3,500). In Hanoi, the same procedure — using the same implant brands — costs significantly less, and that includes our care coordination service. Even after factoring in flights and accommodation, most patients save 50–60% overall.

3. How long will I need to stay in Hanoi?

Most implant procedures require two visits: the first for the implant placement (typically a stay of 5–7 days), and a second visit approximately 3–6 months later for the crown fitting (typically 3–4 days). Our care coordinator will create a personalised timeline based on your treatment plan.

4. Do you offer Japanese-language support?

Absolutely. Our team includes Japanese-speaking care coordinators who will guide you through every step — from your initial online consultation, through your stay in Hanoi, and during any follow-up communication after you return home.

5. What is included in the Implant Vacations Travel package?

Our all-inclusive packages cover the dental treatment itself, airport transfers, hotel accommodation near the clinic, a dedicated care coordinator, translation services, and assistance planning leisure activities during your recovery time. Contact us for a personalised quote tailored to your needs.
